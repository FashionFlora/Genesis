Mutation: replace the fixed motor persistence with two online predictors of visual
intensity change, one for each turn direction. The predictor is updated only from
the sensory change following the organism's own previous turn, then its predicted
advantage biases the next turn. Eye geometry is inferred from n_sensors and the
fixed context-pixel suppression is removed so the mechanism is less tied to one
world layout.
Why: the previous motor trace did not improve rooms in the full v7 ablation. This
tests the missing action -> sensory consequence link directly while keeping the
controller small and local.
