Parents: life_metabolic_context (odkrywca_life) and dynamics_energy_trace (odkrywca_dynamics).

Change: retain the leader's color-by-visual-context eligibility trace, but project
each updated context's preferences onto a zero-sum competition across colors.
An energetic gain for one pursued color therefore lowers the relative allocation
to alternatives instead of growing independent preference weights. This tests
whether finite competitive memory preserves useful metabolic learning while
reducing sensitivity to accumulated weight scale.
