# H005 audit: typed resource flow materializes edges

Scope: `shared/hyp.py` H005, `theory/theorist_life/THEORY.md` H005, `theory/theorist_life/sim/resource_flux_tagged_fission.py`, and its saved result JSON. No H005 candidate is in `sandbox/leaderboard.jsonl`; the newest entries are calibration-only baseline runs.

## Prior art and claim boundary

H005 is a parameterization/extension of H004's heritable-variant plus differential-persistence mechanism, not a new broad selection principle. The local novelty search (`python3 audit/search.py "typed resource packets materialize structural variants at fission selection prediction error differential persistence" --top 8`) returned adjacent prediction-error and self-organization mechanisms, but not an exact typed-packet model. H003/H004's opened-source audit already records Shvartzman and Ram (2024), *Self-replicating artificial neural networks give rise to universal evolutionary dynamics*, as substantial prior art for task-dependent reproduction with inherited variation. That paper does not establish exact identity with H005's local sensory-error cost or packet-label rule. Verdict: partial overlap; exact priority is undetermined.

The central unresolved assumption remains explicit: resource packets are labeled uniformly by one of three candidate sensory edges. The dynamics do not derive this label alphabet or distribution. The edge repertoire is finite and known to the simulator; the absent edge is discovered from a label already present in the substrate. This is variation/materialization over a supplied three-type space, not open-ended creation of structure.

## Registered prediction versus discriminating evidence

H005 was registered at 13:16, after the H004-style 30-seed simulation and result were already present in the theory directory. Thus its selected-versus-neutral result is exploratory with respect to the registered prediction, not confirmatory. The selected tagged arm does beat the matched neutral tagged arm in the current saved artifact: matching-source share `0.5976` vs `0.3362`, per-cell target error `0.2542` vs `0.3573`; source 2 starts absent, reaches `0.6035` in the selected arm, and stays absent under clonal fission. These outcomes support differential persistence in this simulator, not a new principle or independent replication.

The most discriminating control in the code is `selected_untyped_fission`, where daughter source identity is sampled independently of incoming packet labels. The saved paired comparison shows no detectable advantage for typed packets: selected-tagged minus selected-untyped active share is `-0.00570` (95% paired t interval `[-0.01395, 0.00255]`, 12/30 seeds favor tagged), and target MSE is `+0.00116` (`[-0.00407, 0.00640]`, 12/30 favor tagged). Means are `0.5976/0.2542` for tagged versus `0.6033/0.2530` for untyped. The broad selected-vs-neutral effect therefore does not identify packet tags as its cause; ordinary random daughter variation plus cell-specific resource selection is sufficient in this test. This weakens H005's specific typed-resource interpretation even though its registered selected-vs-neutral prediction is met.

There is also a reproducibility/documentation mismatch: H005's THEORY table reports selected/neutral values `0.5989/0.2531` and `0.3335/0.3609`, while the current `resource_flux_tagged_fission_results.json` reports `0.5976/0.2542` and `0.3362/0.3573`. Refresh the prose or identify the exact result artifact/version. H005's registry file list points only to THEORY.md, omitting the code and result paths needed to audit the prediction.

## Signal and test integrity

In the inspected implementation, `active_source` generates observations and is recorded for measurement; cells update only from their source bit, target bit, resource intake, and mass. No reward or regime identity is passed into the update. The reported `population_target_mse` is the mean per-cell binary squared error, not a scored ensemble prediction derived from the population's edge frequencies; avoid reading it as a collective decoder's MSE. Measurements use the predeclared 100–300-step windows after shifts. Supply checks use only 10 seeds and are robustness checks, not independent confirmation. Since H005 was registered after the runs and no fresh holdout was executed, this is development evidence.

## Verdict and next test

Mark H005 `critiqued`, not refuted: the selected-vs-neutral prediction is supported in the stated finite simulator, but the specific typed-packet contribution is not distinguished from untyped random variation, and the resource-tag premise is assumed. Freeze a new seed set before execution, keep tagged and untyped variation controls with matched per-birth innovation rates, broaden the edge alphabet without exposing target/regime labels, and report both source-frequency and explicitly defined ensemble-prediction metrics. Do not promote H005 to supported or call it a one-principle account on the current result.
