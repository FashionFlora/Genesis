# H001 — coactivity-gated probationary sprouting

H001 transitioned from `proposed` to `critiqued` on 2026-10-22. Exact priority of the particular schedule is unresolved; the material principle belongs to established activity-dependent structural plasticity and selectionist synaptic competition.

## Mechanism audit against the one-principle requirement

The implementation uses local evidence, but its dynamics are a hand-specified state machine rather than one law from which all behavior follows:

- `K−1` mature slots plus one permanently reserved probe slot impose the growth cap by construction. `max_total_contacts_observed <= K` is a code invariant, not an emergent result.
- Exploration is introduced separately by uniform random sampling, optionally restricted to neighbors currently coactive with `x`.
- Learning and stabilization use an EMA covariance estimate, an acceptance threshold, and an explicit probation period `P`.
- Pruning uses a different threshold and a separate `D`-step low-score counter. Replacement compares the probe against the weakest stable contact.

Thus the tested version has local growth, a temporary candidate state, local evidence, competition, and pruning, but those pieces do not all derive from coactivity alone. In particular, the single stable/probe slot budget, random search, two score thresholds, timer, and capacity are independently chosen controls. The test does not show neuron growth, general memory consolidation, or sensorimotor exploration. It tests changing synapse occupancy in a finite, fixed neighborhood.

The algorithm does not compute variational/free energy, maximize entropy, or optimize empowerment: there is no belief model, entropy objective, or action/control loop. The coactivity-based selection rule is, however, closely related in broad principle to established Hebbian and selectionist accounts of synaptic change. It resembles the synaptic-selection part of Edelman/Changeux-style theories, not the complete neuronal group selection theory. Fernando, Szathmáry & Husbands (2012) distinguish generalized selectionist descriptions from true Darwinian replicators; H001's individual contact has no copying/heredity mechanism, so it should not be called a Darwinian replicator or new neural-Darwinist law.

## Test audit

Read `sim/probationary_sprouting.py` and `sim/probationary_sprouting_result.json`; I did not rerun the script because it writes into the theory author's folder. The run has 20 seeds, eight target regimes of 1500 steps, `N=8`, and both a null stream and target stream. In the reported `coactive, P=50, K=2` condition, target coverage is `0.8806` (seed SD `0.0316`), mean recovery to 50 consecutive target-containing steps is `179.1`, recovery succeeds in all 160 regime windows, stable-contact precision is `0.9053`, and the null condition has zero stable births per seed on average. A fixed edge covers `0.125`. This supports the narrow tested prediction. At the same P and K, uniform sprouting gives `0.7658` coverage (SD `0.0854`) and recovery `338.6` steps; this is a useful ablation because environment seeds are held independently of the wiring RNG.

The target index is used to generate the correlation regime and calculate metrics, but the contact selection/update functions do not read that index; there is no direct target-ID leakage. The candidate is evaluated only under the balanced, fixed-rate Bernoulli design. The hand-written local covariance score, neutral-rate initialization, thresholds, probation duration, pruning timer, and one-contact probe policy are all part of the algorithm and need sensitivity tests before robustness claims.

The existing `budgeted_competition.py` is a relevant comparator, but current result files do not provide a fully matched comparison: that method globally ranks all eight scores and uses a different RNG stream. Compare it on identical generated observations and tuning budget, while reporting the cost of the global comparison, before claiming the local rule preserves performance. More importantly for the new project direction, vary or derive `K`, `P`, thresholds, and `D`; demonstrate whether the same local dynamical quantity actually controls exploration, stabilization, memory retention, and pruning without those independent policy switches.

## Verdict

H001 is **critiqued**, not marked `known` as an identical algorithm: the precise EMA initialization, probation timer, and threshold sequence were not located in the opened sources. The core mechanism—activity-dependent synapse formation/retention under competition with weakening/elimination—is already known. The recorded code result is supported narrowly for its synthetic regime-shift stream. H001 does not currently meet the orchestrator's requirement for one fundamental principle with low manual rule/constant count.

## Independent replication follow-up

The experimenter independently reimplemented H001, first matching the author's seed to numerical tolerance, then tested 20 new seeds. On the registered balanced stream the result meets every recorded structural threshold: target coverage 0.8514 ± 0.0397, recovery to 50 sustained target-contact steps in 222.9 ± 59.5 steps, independent-null stable contacts/step 0, and maximum contacts 2. The controlled source-only rate shift also passes its separate test. The experimenter accordingly changed H001 to `supported`; I verified the status and result summary.

The evidence supports the registered structural prediction only. The extra readout gives H001 target MSE 0.0373 ± 0.0099, while fixed Hebb gives 0.00353 ± 0.00018 and does not change its structure. Thus H001 has not shown better predictive accuracy than that baseline. Its mean work proxy is also higher (about 1.166 million versus 876 thousand per run). The reported zero target coverage for baselines without a `stable` field is not a valid comparison and should be shown as not applicable. The main structural test has no target-ID/reward/gradient leakage: the regime target is used by the generator/evaluator, while the learner receives source and neighbor observations. A broader common-mode rate-drift diagnostic had worse recovery and false stable contacts, but it changes target identity and source/background rates together and is not the registered IID-null condition.

This replication strengthens the narrow component result without changing the prior-art or one-principle critique. It does not derive the reserved sprout, random sampling, probation timer, stabilization/pruning thresholds, or hard cap from one law.

## Larger-neighborhood follow-up

The experimenter also ran experiments/h001-neighborhood-16/result.json with 16 candidate targets. On the balanced p=.5 condition, H001 target coverage was 0.7306 (SD 0.0551) and recovery was 373.3 (SD 64.2) steps; this is above the random-edge reference of 1/16 but below H001's N=8 registered coverage/recovery bounds. Stable-edge precision was 0.8308 and the two-contact cap still held. On the broader condition that changes both source/target event rates and target identity, coverage was 0.6799 (SD 0.0830) and recovery 371.8 (SD 82.7); this is a useful stress result but not the independent-IID-null condition used by H001's registered prediction. The controlled source-only rate shift with one fixed target remained strong at coverage 0.9738 and recovery 25.7 steps.

At N=16, fixed Hebb still has lower balanced target MSE (0.00350) than H001 (0.06750). This does not overturn H001's supported status for its registered N=8 stream, but it shows that performance and recovery degrade as the neighborhood grows. Report this limitation alongside the narrow support claim; do not generalize the N=8 thresholds to larger neighborhoods.
