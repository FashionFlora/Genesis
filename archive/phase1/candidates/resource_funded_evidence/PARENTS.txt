Mutation/crossover:
Prediction surprise now allocates a normalized, finite resource flow among
source-target processes. The received flow updates each process's conditional
evidence as well as its resource mass; mass-dependent retention, depletion,
and fission therefore couple learning, memory, pruning, and growth.

The mechanism removes an independent per-observation evidence update. It tests
whether resource return can drive both adaptation speed and process survival.
