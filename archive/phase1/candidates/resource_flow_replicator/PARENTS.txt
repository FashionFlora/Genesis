Change: conserve each target's resource across competing source relations, multiplying
each share by its local returned predictive accuracy. The same resulting share weights
the relation update, so depletion, persistence, and learning are coupled without a
separate mutation schedule. This tests whether relative return can replace accumulated
absolute resource and resist stale source mass after rapid regime changes.
