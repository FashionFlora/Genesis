Mutation/crossover:
Each context keeps an active and dormant conditional transition model. Only the
active state learns; when the dormant state better predicts the observed
transition, their roles exchange. This replaces surprise-threshold switching
with predictive comparison. Actions are treated as interventions whose observed
outcome uncertainty drives exploration; no reward, phase, apple, or snake length
is used.
