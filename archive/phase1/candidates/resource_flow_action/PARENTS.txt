Mutation/crossover:
Each source-target relation carries one pair [signed evidence, returned resource].
Prediction surprise determines returned flow; that flow accumulates relational mass,
while the same relation's evidence is updated and selected by mass. In control,
local sensory contact returns flow to action channels; retained action mass selects
the direction with the largest returned resource. No reward, apple channel, snake
length, or separate learning clock is read; all sensory planes contribute only as
an untyped local contact signal.
