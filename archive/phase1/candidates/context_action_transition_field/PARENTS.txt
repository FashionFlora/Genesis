Mutation/crossover:
The action-conditioned transition evidence and the context-addressed causal
support are fused into one field keyed by (context, action, source, target,
source-state). Exponentially retained evidence gives each relation a local
predictive contrast; the same contrast predicts outcomes, recalls a returning
context, and scores the intervention with least evidence. This removes the
parent's separate action-change statistic and makes action selection use the
same sufficient state as prediction.
