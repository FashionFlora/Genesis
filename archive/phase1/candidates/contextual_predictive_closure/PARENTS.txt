Mutation/crossover:
The separate context-action evidence and causal support are replaced by one
sparse transition field. A field entry is the sufficient state for recurrence,
prediction, and intervention. `act()` chooses the action with the smallest
expected next-state description (binary predictive entropy); an unseen tie is
resolved by the current sensory address, not by a schedule, reward, apple, or
snake length. This tests whether self-prediction alone can supply action.
