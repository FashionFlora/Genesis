The returned predictive flow still updates both signed evidence and relation
resource. Unlike the parents' fixed or uniform retention, each resource
denominator includes the same squared prediction loss that generated the
flow. Accurate processes therefore retain resource, while inaccurate ones
dissipate it and relinquish selection without a separate pruning rule.
