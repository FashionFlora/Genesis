Mutation/crossover:
The contact-action sparse substrate now stores a per-contact reserve. Each
update retains the reserve itself, funds the signed relation with prediction
flow, and lets the same reserve weight source competition. Action reserve is
returned only when the chosen move reaches its proposed cell; local free-space
flow then selects the next action. No apple, reward, snake length, regime, or
fixed decay clock is read.
