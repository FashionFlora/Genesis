The parent's predictive returned-flow relation is unchanged for stream learning.
Action resource is now addressed by the proposed destination cell. A chosen
contact returns mass only when the next observation reaches that destination;
the same retained field mass then weights future local contacts. The action is
therefore a consequence of resource persistence and observed transition, with
no apple, length, reward, or fixed movement strategy.
