Action-conditioned outcome evidence is retained in separate context-addressed
predictors, so a returning context retrieves its own intervention evidence
instead of pooling it with another regime. The action rule continues the
parent's uncertainty-driven exploration, now querying uncertainty in the
context-specific consequence model. This preserves causal evidence across
context recurrence; it does not assume that lower observations are better.
