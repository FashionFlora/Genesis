Mutation: predictive context still routes histories to local transition models,
but model exchange is selected from the aggregate error on the complete next
sensory vector. One context therefore recalls a joint causal model as a unit;
the update goes to the selected model only. This tests whether joint outcome
coherence is a better retrieval signal than independent channel exchanges.
