Mutation: contact_lag_growth's sparse, demand-triggered topology now uses the
resource_flow_memory idea that predictive return is the sole currency. A new
edge is admitted only when its observed lag relation exceeds finite-sample
uncertainty; its return above an uninformed predictor funds persistence, and
the same deficit consumes the edge. This removes an independent fixed decay.
The edge remains the sole internal unit for its target.
