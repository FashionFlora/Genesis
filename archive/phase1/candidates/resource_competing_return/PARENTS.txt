Mutation/crossover:
The resource_flow_memory update gave every source relation the same target-only
return, leaving resource mass behaviorally inert. This version computes each
source's conditional prediction before the target arrives, then divides one
finite target return among the competing source relations in proportion to
their predictive fit. The same returned share updates relation evidence and
retained mass, and mass-weighted evidence selects the predictor. This tests
whether source-specific resource competition can outperform a direct
coactivity selector without an action or reward rule.
