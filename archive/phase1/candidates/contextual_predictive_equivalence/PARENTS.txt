Parents: candidates/predictive_context_sufficiency/principle.py

Derivation:
The principle treats a context as a predictive equivalence class: histories
that give different next-step distributions must keep separate local evidence.
The address is a permutation-invariant weighted summary of the current sensory
state, and the global model is only the low-evidence fallback. This removes
the privileged sensor-half address from the parent while retaining one law:
internal distinctions persist exactly to the extent that they predict.
