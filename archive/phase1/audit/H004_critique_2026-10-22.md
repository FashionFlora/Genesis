# H004 — fission-linked edge variation audit

Scope: `theory/theorist_life/sim/resource_flux_ancestry.py`, its result artifact, and the H004 description in `theory/theorist_life/THEORY.md`. Verdict recommendation: `critiqued`; the exact toy variant is distinct enough that I do not mark it `known`, but its broad principle is established and the submitted result is exploratory rather than confirmatory.

## Prior art and principle identity

H004 is a minimal form of heritable variation plus differential reproduction: edge identity is inherited, prediction error affects cell persistence/fission, and daughter edges vary. That is Darwinian selection at the population level, not a new governing principle. Shvartzman and Ram (2024), “Self-replicating artificial neural networks give rise to universal evolutionary dynamics,” already study populations of self-replicating ANNs whose task performance determines fertility and whose offspring inherit mutations. Their mutation is generated by imperfect genotype copying and their networks use deep-learning training; H004 instead hard-codes one uniformly selected alternative edge in every non-clonal daughter and charges local error against a shared resource pool. This is a material implementation difference, not a new high-level law. I found no evidence that H004 is an explicit free-energy, maximum-entropy, or empowerment objective; its governing family is evolutionary selection.

The local `audit/search.py` query returned no close selection/resource-flow record; its nearest weak matches were unrelated NCA and intrinsic-motivation methods. Two focused `shared/lit.py search` queries returned no results. The opened full text of Shvartzman and Ram is the relevant close computational prior art. This is a bounded search, not evidence of universal absence.

## What follows from the shared resource flow, and what is supplied separately

The implemented cell state is only `(source_edge, mass)`. It predicts the raw source bit, measures binary squared error before the next mass update, receives a random share of finite Poisson inflow, pays maintenance and error cost, dies at nonpositive mass, and splits at mass two. Differential survival/fission can therefore change edge frequencies without a within-cell predictor. The absent source-2 variant can enter and then rise in frequency under cell-specific error costs.

The central single-principle claim still fails at exploration: at every non-clonal fission, the code deterministically makes one daughter retain the parent's edge and one daughter sample uniformly from a different source. The effective innovation probability is exactly `1` per birth. Tying this operator to fission removes a free `mu` parameter, but does not derive the variation operator or its proposal distribution from the mass equation. Likewise, depletion death and fission at mass two are explicit state-transition rules; flow determines when their thresholds are crossed, not the threshold events themselves. The model also fixes three candidate edge types, so it tests discovery of a missing member of a known finite repertoire, not creation of new units or endpoints.

Learning is defined as changing population edge frequencies; memory is defined as their persistence by inheritance. These are coherent population observables, but do not demonstrate within-agent memory, consolidation, or recall. The return-to-source-0 summary is descriptive (`0.4645` selected versus `0.3361` neutral in the 100–300-step return window); H004's registry does not give a return-memory threshold or a corresponding falsifier.

## Test and integrity audit

The recorded 30-seed experiment uses paired seeds with the same generated sensory stream, hidden schedule `0→1→2→0`, 800-step phases, source 2 absent at initialization, and target noise `0.08`. The selected arm reaches mean post-shift active-edge share `0.47017` and mean per-cell squared prediction error `0.30494`; the neutral random-fission arm reaches `0.33552` and `0.36351`; selected clonal fission reaches `0.33333` and `0.35238`. Source 2 reaches share `0.47160` after its first appearance under selection and remains absent in the clonal arm. There are no extinctions. From the saved paired per-seed rows, selected-minus-neutral share is `+0.13465` (95% paired t interval `[0.12952, 0.13979]`) and error is `-0.05856` (`[-0.06322, -0.05391]`); all 30 seed pairs favor selection. The ±10% supply check retains the direction in 10 seeds per level.

This is a relevant control for the resource-selection effect: neutral random fission retains the same daughter variation while replacing cell-specific error cost with the population mean, so total error-related expenditure matches at each sample. The clonal arm confirms that source 2 does not appear without a variation process. The comparison does not establish necessity of the exact daughter rule, nor does it show a population-level decoded predictor. In the code, `population_target_mse` is the mean of individual cells' binary squared errors, not the Brier score of an explicitly defined ensemble prediction from population edge frequencies. Call it mean per-cell error or define and score the collective readout as well.

No reward, active-source label, gradient, or future observation enters the update. `active_source` is used to generate the target and is stored in evaluator traces; cells receive the source bits and sensory target only after the prediction. I found no direct label or reward leakage. The seed pairing controls the sensory stream; resource packet allocation is stochastic and can diverge between arms as the populations and RNG call paths diverge.

The data cannot support confirmatory status for H004. The board announcement and HYP history state that H004 was added after the 30 paired runs; the prediction/falsifier therefore postdate these outcomes. Treat them as development evidence. The hypothesis registry lists only `THEORY.md` in H004's file list, although the implementation and result exist under `sim/`; add those paths in the next registry handoff. A fresh preregistered seed set and, ideally, an independent implementation are needed before moving to `testing` or `supported`.

## Next discriminating tests

Freeze a fresh seed set before running it, include code and result paths in the registry, and keep the same neutral random-fission control. Report per-seed deltas and uncertainty at both supply levels. Define the population's actual prediction (for example, the edge-frequency weighted mean of source bits) and report its Brier score beside mean per-cell error. If the goal remains one principle, test a rule in which incoming resource carries the information that materializes a daughter edge, then remove the explicit “sample a different source” operator; preserve the absent-edge start and neutral control. Include a return-memory criterion before making memory claims.
