# Growth rules from runs

- `growth_metabolic_graph` organism-v3 reached score 0.046875 and grew to about 492 reported units; its short instrumentation showed that removing pruning increased persistent units (229 vs 106 at step 1500), but did not test score causality.
- `growth_signed_trace` organism-v4 achieved score 2.3076171875, survival 0.92285, mean energy 0.79012, and reversal latency 247.16. After correcting the structure report to return per-organism counts, active units ranged 0 -> max 35.746 -> final 34.623 with no violations of the 302-unit limit. This is below the 2.840820 leader. Its earlier growth parent scored 0.046875 on organism-v3, so that cross-version difference is not evidence of a mutation effect.
- A short 64-step smoke test cannot measure learned retention or pruning: initial exposure activates structures immediately, and the window is shorter than the full reversal/foraging task.
- `growth_audit.py` ran four 6000-step, batch-8 organism-v4 trajectories on seed 31. On the base room scale, pruning-on ended at 36.875 active units/organism, 2.375 mean rooms, 0.875 survival, 0.845 mean energy, and 0.816 cumulative post-reversal correct bites. The matched no-pruning ablation ended at 48 units, 2.875 rooms, 1.0 survival, 0.830 energy, and 0.849 correct bites. This single-seed audit suggests pruning reduced task performance; it is not a full-judge score comparison.
- Estimated allocated state was 1203 bytes/organism for both pruning conditions after initial growth. The batched implementation shares a dense slot array across organisms, so clearing an organism's `exists` bit does not release storage while any other row retains that feature. Logical pruning did not save allocated memory.
- A 2x-room-side run of `growth_signed_trace` had 10.5 units/organism at step 1024, then no active units by 2048; all organisms died before 4096. This is evidence of poor scale robustness, from one seed only. Candidate `n_units` is an ignored upper bound in this implementation, so varying it would not test world size.
- On this seed, the parent `growth_metabolic_graph` ended with zero survivors and zero active units by step 6000. This local paired run does not replace a full matched-seed judge comparison.
- The audit measures realized world outcomes, not a standalone prediction loss. Its cumulative post-reversal bite fraction is a behavioral proxy. Connections are one-to-one with live feature nodes in this design, not an independent sparse edge topology.
- `growth_delayed_support` organism-v4 scored 2.3125 over 1024 worlds, below the 2.840820 leader. Its reported live units/connections stayed at 0 through step 6000, at 302, 1k, 10k, and 100k upper bounds. The delayed-support gate therefore prevented structure from forming; this run cannot show memory savings or scale robustness.
- The original delayed-support implementation immediately compacted newly observed, unsupported features, erasing `previous_active` before the next energy change could credit support. Preserving one-step pending candidates allowed growth: seed 31, batch 8, 6000 steps ended with 17.375 active units/organism and 48 shared slots (7104 tensor bytes); a threshold-1e6 no-growth ablation ended at 0 units and 23 transient shared slots (3504 bytes). This demonstrates growth but also shows growth increases memory; it does not establish prediction benefit.
- In that matched seed, both normal and no-growth conditions reached mean room 2.25 with survival 0.875. Doubling room side caused extinction by step 4096 (survival 0), so delayed growth has no demonstrated scale robustness. Do not interpret allocated shared slots as per-organism brain size.
